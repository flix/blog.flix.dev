## Will Large Language Models (LLMs) Aid or Hinder Adoption of New Programming Languages

I am PL researcher working on compilers, type and effect systems, program
analysis. I am also the lead developer of the Flix programming language
(flix.dev). I am not a AI researcher.

Reccently, at work, on HackerNews, and on Reddit I have seen quesations to the effect of:

> Will large langauge models aid or hinder adaoption of new programming
> languages? 

In this blog post, I will discuss this question from the viewpoint of a PL
researcher.
